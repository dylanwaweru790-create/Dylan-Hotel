Culinary Philosophy & Heritage

Simple ingredients, honest hospitality, and the timeless warmth of wood fire.

Founded in 2018 on Market Street, Luminère was born from a desire to bring back unhurried dining. We work directly with independent coastal fishermen in Half Moon Bay and organic family orchards across Sonoma and Marin counties.

Every morning begins with rolling silky sheets of fresh egg pasta and feeding our 48-hour sourdough starter. By late afternoon, aged white oak logs are brought to ember in our custom stone hearth.
